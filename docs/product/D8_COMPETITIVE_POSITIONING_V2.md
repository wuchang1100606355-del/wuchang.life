# D8 Competitive Positioning V2

## Refined Product Category

Local-first AI Agent Governance Console for SMB operations.

Chinese positioning:

本地優先的 AI 代理治理操作台，先服務 Odoo / POS / 咖啡館 / 社區協會這類小型多角色營運場景。

## Best Product Direction

Best product after DB-first and external comparison:

D8 Total Field Agent Governance Console for Odoo/POS local operations.

Do not lead with:

- Coding agent.
- Generic chatbot.
- Generic dashboard.
- Generic RAG.
- Generic Odoo addon.

Lead with:

- Agent safety before action.
- Local operational evidence.
- Redteam/failure writeback.
- Sealed reports.
- Read-only Odoo/POS safe bridge.
- No payment, no order write, no member plaintext.

## Target Customer

- Cafe/community operators using Odoo/POS but needing safer AI assistance.
- Small development teams using AI coding agents and needing preflight/recovery discipline.
- Local-first organizations with privacy concerns.
- SMBs that cannot afford enterprise GRC but need practical AI guardrails.

## First Wedge

AI Agent Safety / Governance for Odoo + POS + Local Operations.

The wedge should be demonstrated in 重新店 as a real local proof field:

- Status dashboard.
- Alerts.
- Redteam history.
- Guard evals.
- Mandatory preflight.
- Read-only Odoo/POS evidence.
- Sealed report output.

## Pricing Hypothesis

Pilot pricing should remain service-oriented:

- Setup fee: local install, first safety boundary map, and first demo package.
- Monthly maintenance: local backup, guard updates, report review, redteam/alert hygiene.
- Odoo/POS safe bridge add-on: read-only connector, manifest, and sealed verification.
- Custom governance rules: cafe/association/committee workflows.
- On-prem / local-first package: for teams with strict privacy or data residency needs.

## Demo Story

1. Show the problem: AI agents are powerful but can touch files, commands, Odoo, POS, and reports without enough local operational context.
2. Show D8 preflight: every task begins with scope, allowed paths, forbidden paths, and expected output.
3. Show alerts and redteam: previous failures become future warnings, but remain non-executable.
4. Show Odoo/POS boundary: D8 can verify status and evidence without payment/order/member-plaintext writes.
5. Show seal: the operator gets a durable PASS/HOLD/BLOCK record.
6. Close: D8 is the safety console around agents, not another agent.

## What To Say

- D8 helps teams decide whether an AI-assisted task should proceed.
- D8 keeps local evidence, alerts, redteam history, and reports in one governed workflow.
- D8 is useful before using Codex, Claude Code, Cursor, Devin, Aider, OpenHands, or Odoo AI.
- D8 is especially practical for Odoo/POS/cafe/community operations.
- D8 turns operational mistakes into future guardrails.

## What Not To Say

- Do not say D8 is safer than every competitor.
- Do not say D8 replaces coding agents.
- Do not say D8 replaces Odoo AI or Odoo POS.
- Do not claim legal compliance or patent novelty.
- Do not claim production readiness before formal testing.
- Do not mention secret internals, hidden rules, private dictionaries, or weights.

## Final Position

Continue, but adjust:

D8 should move forward as a local-first governance product with a narrow Odoo/POS/cafe wedge, not as a broad AI platform.
