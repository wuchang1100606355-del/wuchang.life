# D8 Forbidden Claims

## Do Not Claim

- D8 is absolutely safe.
- D8 is unbreakable.
- D8 is legally compliant by default.
- D8 is patent-protected or novel before counsel review.
- D8 replaces Codex, Claude Code, Cursor, Devin, Aider, OpenHands, Odoo AI, or Odoo POS.
- D8 is production-ready before formal tests and release gates.
- D8 can place orders in the MVP.
- D8 can capture payments in the MVP.
- D8 can read member plaintext in the MVP.
- Redteam records are executable automation.
- Secret internals, hidden rules, private dictionaries, weights, or WHY_IT_RUNS can be publicly disclosed.

## Required Safe Language

- Local-first governance console.
- Agent safety layer before action.
- Read-only Odoo/POS evidence in MVP.
- Sealed reports and recovery handoff.
- Candidate patent areas require counsel review.
- Product is ready for local pilot packaging, not production release.
