## Product Name
Sovereign Edge POS Ops Layer

Chinese name: 本地主權 POS 維運層

## One-Line Promise
讓 Odoo 店家使用 AI 輔助點餐、通知與客顯時，AI 只能提出候選，本地節點負責還原、驗證、留證，避免亂寫帳、誤發通知與店內斷網混亂。

## Who It Is For
- Odoo-based cafe, quick-service, or small restaurant operators.
- Stores that want AI assistance without letting cloud AI directly control POS or accounting.
- Stores that need evidence-backed operation during degraded network or multi-device situations.

## What It Does In MVP
- Converts voice/text/order intent into POS candidates, not live orders.
- Routes LINE WORKS notification drafts through local reconstruction and staff approval.
- Keeps customer display and XiaoJ TV output as auditable display plans until reviewed.
- Blocks market demo if duplicate POS identity, missing ROI metrics, or untrusted notification/display paths are found.

## What It Does Not Claim Yet
- It does not replace Toast, Square, Odoo, Clover, Lightspeed, TouchBistro, Shopify POS, SpotOn, or Revel.
- It does not process payments.
- It does not directly write Odoo, DB, LINE WORKS, Tailscale, or Chrome display state.
- It does not use unverified ancient-math mappings as buyer-facing proof.

## Merchant Outcomes To Prove
- Faster staff confirmation from AI-generated candidates.
- Fewer wrong-store or duplicate-POS target mistakes.
- Safer notification workflow with approval and replay.
- More credible degraded-network operations story.
- Better audit replay after a disputed order, notification, or display action.

## Demo Stopline
If the demo cannot show candidate-only flow, local reconstruction, evidence replay, and runtime HOLD before action, the product must not be marketed as launch-ready.
