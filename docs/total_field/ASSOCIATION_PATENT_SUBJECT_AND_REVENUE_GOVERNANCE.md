## Academic-Institution-Style Principle

The intended model follows the public-interest spirit commonly seen in academic or institutional technology transfer:

- Individual invention contribution is recognized.
- The inventor states that the patent could be developed privately without public-interest conversion, and that association-centered public-interest conversion is voluntary rather than required for personal benefit.
- Institution-centered ownership or stewardship is documented by written agreement.
- Revenue is governed by transparent rules rather than private ad hoc disposal.
- Formal filing, assignment, licensing, remuneration, tax, and accounting details require professional review.

Reference basis for review:

- Taiwan Patent Act, Article 7 ownership concepts for employee-duty invention, funded research, contract allocation, remuneration, and inventor name attribution.
- Existing Total Field strategy document: `docs/strategy/wuchang_sovereign_economic_engine_v8_zh.md`.

## Legal-Personhood And General-Assembly Gate

The updated human governance boundary is:

```text
新任理事長將在下一次大會前完成法人化準備；在大會中，才正式與協會締約並完成捐贈或承接安排。未法人化前，協會不得被描述為已能承接專利權、專利收益、捐贈或長期資料治理資產。
```

Safe interpretation:

- Before legal personhood and assembly approval: record intent, preparation, evidence, and draft governance only.
- Before legal personhood, a letter of intent may be signed if it clearly says it is preparatory, does not transfer patent rights, does not receive donation or money, and is subject to legal-personhood readiness, assembly approval, written contract, patent attorney review, and accounting review.
- At the general assembly: use member/board-approved procedure to authorize contract, donation, assignment, license, or revenue governance.
- After legal-personhood readiness and signed documents: public wording may be updated from "研議 / 準備 / 意向" to the documented legal status.
- If legal personhood is delayed, all homepage and product wording must remain in preparation language.

## Letter Of Intent Safe Terms

If signed before legal personhood, the letter of intent should include:

- Parties and identity: inventor / new chair / preparatory association representatives.
- Purpose: prepare for association-centered patent and technology revenue governance.
- Non-transfer boundary: no patent assignment, no donation completed, no revenue receipt, no permanent custody before legal personhood and assembly approval.
- Future conditions: legal-personhood readiness, general assembly approval, written definitive agreement, patent attorney review, accounting review, and conflict-of-interest disclosure.
- Evidence boundary: the LOI may be used as governance evidence, not as proof that the association already owns the patent or received donation.
- AI boundary: AI can draft and check text only; humans and professionals approve.

Safe LOI sentence:

```text
本意向書僅確認雙方於協會法人化與大會授權後，研議締結正式捐贈、讓與、授權或收益治理契約之意向；於正式契約簽署並完成必要審查前，不發生專利權移轉、捐贈完成、收益承接或長期資產保管效果。
```

## Required Formalization Before Any Claim

Before any homepage, pitch deck, contract, filing, or public claim says the association is the actual patent owner, all of the following must be completed:

1. Optional pre-legal-personhood letter of intent that is explicitly preparatory and non-transfer.
2. Legal-personhood readiness sufficient for receiving rights, signing contracts, and maintaining accountable records.
3. General assembly or association-authorized resolution.
4. Written inventor-to-association donation, assignment, joint ownership, or license agreement.
5. Patent attorney review of filing entity, inventor list, claims, assignment timing, and publication risk.
6. Accountant or tax advisor review of future patent revenue, donation, reimbursement, royalty, or service-fee treatment.
7. Conflict-of-interest disclosure and recusal rule for any director, developer, vendor, or store operator who may benefit.
8. Evidence package tying invention contribution to dated packets, reports, sealed records, and source manifests.

Until those are complete, the safe wording is:

```text
發明人兼新任理事長已表達願意以協會公益治理為中心，於法人化與下一次大會程序完成後，研議由協會作為專利與技術收益治理主體；正式權利歸屬、捐贈、讓與、授權與收益分配，仍須經法人化條件、協會決議、書面契約、專利代理人與會計專業審查後生效。
```

## Revenue Governance Rule

Patent and technology revenue may support:

- Association-owned hardware upgrade.
- Association local vault and governed backup.
- Community digital public-interest services.
- Store operation recovery only when documented as mission-related support.
- Training, documentation, and community AI literacy.

Revenue must not be used as:

- Unrecorded private cash.
- Undocumented donation claim.
- Unauthorized franchise or training fee.
- Patent or donation receipt before legal-personhood and assembly authorization.
- Hidden personal consideration for the inventor, chair, developer, store operator, or vendor unless fully disclosed, approved, contracted, and accounted for.
- AI-decided payment.
- Public assertion of guaranteed patent allowance, subsidy approval, or commercial success.

## Homepage Suitability Rule

Homepage copy should be mission-first:

- Lead with the association's community digital public-interest mission.
- Explain public interest as the logical closure of the invention, not as a slogan.
- Define the intent field as community mutual love and mutual support turned into safe action.
- Explain home as the protected harbor and community as the force that protects home.

- Explain the cafe as a field practice and livelihood-recovery node.

- Avoid claiming nonprofit status, patent ownership, tax deduction, or Google eligibility beyond documented facts.

## Forbidden Claims

Do not claim:

- The patent is already granted unless it is officially granted.
- The association is already the legal patent owner before formal assignment or filing evidence exists.
- Google, LINE, Odoo, OpenAI, or any vendor endorses the project unless documented.
- Donations, patent revenue, franchise fees, or training fees are tax-deductible without professional confirmation.
- AI can autonomously approve legal, accounting, payment, POS, or member governance decisions.
