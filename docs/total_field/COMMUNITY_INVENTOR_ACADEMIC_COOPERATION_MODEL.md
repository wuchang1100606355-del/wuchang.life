# Community-Inventor Academic Cooperation Model

Status: governance draft / not legal advice  
Purpose: define an academic-style cooperation route between the Wuchang community public-interest system and the individual inventor/social worker.

## Core Position

The safest academic route is not a direct private transfer between the community and the inventor. The better structure is a three-party cooperation model:

```text
Individual inventor / social worker / system architect
  + Community development association / public-interest field owner
  + University, college, lab, research center, or academic reviewer
  -> Academic public-interest field lab
```

In this model:

- The individual keeps inventor attribution, research contribution records, and technical authorship evidence.
- The community keeps field governance, resident/public-interest purpose, and association decision authority.
- The academic party provides method, research ethics, documentation, student training, evaluation, publication discipline, and industry-academia contract structure.
- XiaoJ / Total Field remains a governance and evidence engine, not a legal signer or financial decision-maker.

## Why Academic Style Fits

The academic cooperation model matches the system's mission because the project is not only a product. It is also:

- community care research,
- social work practice infrastructure,
- elder participation and aging-in-community design,
- AI governance and privacy-preserving system design,
- public-interest commerce and local economic circulation,
- Odoo / POS / property / volunteer delivery applied system research.

The academic route lets the community and the inventor cooperate without collapsing identity, authority, data custody, money, and patent rights into one private arrangement.

## Legal And Policy Anchors To Review

These anchors must be reviewed by humans and professionals before execution:

- Patent Act Article 7: funded R&D and employee-duty inventions depend heavily on written contract allocation; inventor-name attribution remains important.
- Ministry of Education industry-academia cooperation rules: schools may cooperate with civil organizations on R&D, technical services, training, patent application, technology transfer, innovation incubation, and IP use, and written contracts should define deliverables, resources, IP, revenue use, name/logo use, confidentiality, and conflict of interest.
- Research result ownership rules commonly use fairness, contribution, funding, professional capacity, public benefit, and contract allocation as review factors.
- Community development policy frames community development as residents' common needs, mutual aid, administrative support, technical guidance, resource use, and improved quality of life.
- Association and people-group governance requires charter, meetings, accounting, authority basis, and, where needed, legal-personhood readiness.

## Recommended Cooperation Forms

### 1. Preparatory Academic MOU

Use when the association is still preparing formal legal-personhood, IP custody, or finance windows.

Purpose:

- record intent,
- define field-lab scope,
- preserve inventor attribution,
- define no-transfer boundary,
- avoid early money/IP custody claims.

Hard boundary:

```text
No patent transfer.
No donation completion.
No revenue receipt.
No permanent data custody.
No public claim that the association already owns the invention.
```

### 2. Community Public-Interest Field Lab Agreement

Use when the community provides real field context for research, design, testing, education, and evaluation.

Recommended title:

```text
Wuchang 8D Sovereign AI Community XiaoJ Public-Interest Field Lab
```

Scope:

- social work governance workflow,
- caregiver employee workflow,
- elder active participation,
- property/community service routing,
- public-interest commerce and volunteer delivery,
- privacy-preserving intent packets,
- Odoo/POS/accounting evidence prototypes.

### 3. University Or College Industry-Academia Cooperation

Use when a school formally joins.

The school contract should define:

- research subject and deliverables,
- necessary funding, equipment, field access, or service resources,
- IP ownership and use,
- revenue use and distribution,
- name/logo use,
- confidentiality,
- conflict-of-interest handling,
- sensitive technology and human-subject ethics review where applicable.

The individual inventor can participate as:

- inventor / technical author,
- social work practice designer,
- community field architect,
- external expert,
- lecturer or workshop designer,
- technical service provider,
- research collaborator,
- patent or technology source contributor.

### 4. Public-Interest License From Inventor To Association

Use when the inventor wants residents and the association to use the system without immediate private extraction.

Possible structure:

```text
Inventor grants association a limited, revocable or conditional public-interest license
for local resident service, social work governance, caregiver workflow, and community field-lab use.
```

Must define:

- local resident free-use scope,
- association governance scope,
- whether sublicensing is allowed,
- whether commercial pilots require separate approval,
- maintenance and security obligations,
- inventor attribution,
- patent filing boundary,
- termination and rollback.

### 5. Sponsored Research / Technical Service Agreement

Use when the community, school, or third party funds development.

Must define:

- whether funding is salary, service fee, research funding, reimbursement, donation, license fee, or equipment support,
- what belongs to the inventor,
- what belongs to the association,
- what belongs to the school,
- what remains open/public,
- what remains confidential,
- how revenue returns to the community fund pool.

## Required Governance Gates

Before public claims, contract signing, IP assignment, or money flow:

1. Conflict-of-interest disclosure if the inventor is also an association officer or decision-maker.
2. Board or general-assembly authorization where required.
3. Written agreement reviewed by humans.
4. Patent attorney review for patent filing, ownership, assignment, licensing, and disclosure timing.
5. Accountant review for donation, service fee, salary, reimbursement, license revenue, fund-pool accounting, and tax treatment.
6. Data protection review for residents, elders, social work cases, caregiver records, location, health, family risk, and member data.
7. Human-subject or ethics review if academic publication or research involving people is planned.
8. No-delete evidence package: packet, hash, source refs, decision record, and rollback path.

## Forbidden Shortcuts

- Do not frame the cooperation as the community automatically owning the inventor's work without a signed agreement.
- Do not frame the inventor as secretly extracting community assets.
- Do not publish resident, elder, social work, caregiver, property, or merchant sensitive data as research material.
- Do not let AI approve contract, patent ownership, payment, donation, accounting, or human-subject research.
- Do not let commercial partners use the community brand, field data, or XiaoJ identity without written scope.
- Do not turn happiness coin, ticket, or community point into investment language.
