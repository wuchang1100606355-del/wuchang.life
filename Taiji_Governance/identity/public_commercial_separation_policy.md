# Public / Commercial Separation Policy

## Iron Rule

Public-interest assets, community assets, fund-pool resources, and entrusted resources must not be converted into private capital gain.

## Separation

| Scope | Data | Accounting | Authority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Public-interest association | association governance data | association/fund-pool accounting | community governance |
| Community industry | community service operations | separated branch/service records | governed public-interest policy |
| Private cafe | private business records | private commercial accounting | private operator only |
| Technology sponsor | donation/lending/transfer records | support record, not asset control | no public-asset control |

Any private/public data merge requires tensor classification, accounting review, audit, rollback, and human decision.
