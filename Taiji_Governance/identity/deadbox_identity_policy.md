# Deadbox Identity Policy

Identity confusion enters Tensor Deadbox when:

- public-interest asset is requested for private gain
- private cafe authority controls association asset
- sponsor role claims ownership of public asset
- runtime operator issues credential
- commercial income is presented as fund-pool income without accounting separation
- old authority packet is replayed under a stronger identity

Deadbox recovery requires human decision, audit review, authority regeneration, and accounting review when money/assets are involved.
