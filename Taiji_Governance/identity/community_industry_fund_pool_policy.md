# Community Industry Fund-Pool Policy

Community industry may support public-interest funding goals only through auditable records.

## Rules

- no private capital gain distribution
- accounting separation required
- Odoo branch/company/customer separation required
- fund-pool purpose must be explicit
- every inflow/outflow needs audit metadata
- accounting window required for formal finance decisions

AI may propose fund-pool service structures but may not finalize finance, tax, payment, or distribution decisions.
