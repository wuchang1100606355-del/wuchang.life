## Design Intent

The project exists to demonstrate that intelligence can serve public life without relying on public fundraising.

The founder's goal is not equal financial return.

The minimum personal requirement is two meals.

The remaining value generated from business, patent technology, and POS prototype visibility is directed toward community education and elder support.

## Civic Education Role

The system should teach:

1. how community money should be visible and accountable
2. how technology can serve elders and residents
3. how local business can support public value
4. how patents can become community infrastructure
5. how AI governance must avoid plaintext, secret leakage, and uncontrolled authority
6. how civic responsibility can be built step by step

## Landing Path

Chongxin store POS prototype
→ public attention
→ patent technology demonstration
→ technical licensing / consulting / deployment opportunity
→ founder-side revenue
→ Community Digital Development Reserve
→ civic education and elder support

## Boundary

This is not a donation campaign.
This is not token funding.
This is not public investment solicitation.
This is not a live payment coin.

This is a pre-circulation, self-funded, patent-backed civic system prototype.
