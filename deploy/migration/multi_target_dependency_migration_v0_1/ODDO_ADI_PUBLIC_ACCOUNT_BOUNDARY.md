# Odoo ADI Public Account Boundary

版本：2026-05-12

## Rule

General accounting entries are permissioned and auditable.

Public-interest accounts are cloud-published, public, and visible 24H.

## Public 24H Account May Show

- public-interest income summary
- public-interest expense categories
- fund-pool retained amount
- service outcome summary
- ESG non-sensitive indicators
- vector data license income summary

## Public 24H Account Must Not Show

- member plaintext
- individual service records
- merchant confidential data
- supplier unpublished prices
- secrets or credentials
- individual POS transaction identifiers
- reversible behavior vectors
