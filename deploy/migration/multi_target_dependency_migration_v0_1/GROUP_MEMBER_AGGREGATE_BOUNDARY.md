# Group Member Aggregate Boundary

版本：2026-05-12

## Rule

Group member individual information is not visible.

Public view may show:

- formula
- field definition
- aggregation method
- final statistics

Public view must not show:

- individual group member records
- group member to metric mapping
- single merchant revenue details
- single transaction records
- small-sample reversible statistics

## Principle

Show the math.

Hide the people and entities.

Publish the aggregate.

Protect the source database.
