# Non-Sensitive Vector Data Product Boundary

版本：2026-05-11

## Allowed

The association may design a data licensing product for:

- deidentified five-metric vector indicators
- aggregate community service statistics
- ESG indicators
- public-interest academic research outputs

## Blocked

Do not sell or license:

- member plaintext
- individual behavior logs
- reversible identifiers
- member vault data
- business secrets
- committee sensitive minutes
- secrets or credentials

## Buyer ESG Use

Buyers may use licensed data for verifiable public-interest and ESG reporting
only under contract.

Buyer must not:

- reidentify persons
- profile individuals
- resell raw data
- use data for discrimination, credit, insurance, employment, or surveillance
- make unverifiable ESG claims
