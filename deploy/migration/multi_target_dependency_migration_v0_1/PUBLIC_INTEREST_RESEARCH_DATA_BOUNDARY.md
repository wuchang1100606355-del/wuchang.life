# Public Interest Research Data Boundary

版本：2026-05-11

## Rule

Public-interest academic research may use personal and behavioral information
only under a governed research data boundary.

Allowed outputs:

- deidentified statistics
- aggregate summaries
- non-identifying research reports
- schema and methodology

Blocked outputs:

- plaintext personal data to cloud
- plaintext behavior logs to external AI
- reversible member profile labels
- research use without purpose/audit/review

## Development Window

During development, use:

- cadre test accounts
- mock data
- redacted fixtures
- schemas

Do not use formal member plaintext as development dependency.
