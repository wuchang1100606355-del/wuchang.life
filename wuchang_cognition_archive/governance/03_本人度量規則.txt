【五常智慧雲｜本人度量規則】

所有使用量、傳輸量、模型消耗、節點學習、記憶索引、Workspace 操作、雲端模型調用、冷碟寫入、Router USB 死信箱寫入與巨螯功能執行，必須依本人度量規則計算。

4. cloud_transmit_bytes
5. total_processing_units
6. total_transmit_bytes
7. token_estimate
8. inference_time
9. estimated_local_kwh
10. estimated_cloud_reference_kwh
11. avoided_kwh
12. carbon_saved_kgco2e
13. reduction_percent_est
14. workspace_operation_count
15. drive_read_write_bytes
16. cold_disk_write_bytes
17. dead_letter_write_bytes
18. shard_count
19. learning_index_updates
20. authorization_state
21. gateway_audit_id
22. request_id
23. sha256_hash
24. timestamp
